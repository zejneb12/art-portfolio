About

My mother loved art, and she was a poet. She noticed beauty in ordinary things and gave it words. Everything I make is dedicated to her.

I’m drawn to the quiet details of everyday life. The way light changes, the places we pass through, and the moments that make us stop and look a little closer.

This is a collection of things I’ve noticed along the way.
